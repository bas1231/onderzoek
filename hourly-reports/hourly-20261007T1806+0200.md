### Galaxy Research — Polymarket trader-behavior onchain study
Source: Galaxy Research, 2026-10-01, "The Behavior of Polymarket Traders: An analysis of 2.9 million accounts".
- 69.2% of the study's human-paced/retail-classified accounts finished below break-even; aggregate P&L was -$338.9m.
- The 125,429 accounts classified as automated by the study (>50 orders per active day heuristic) finished +$246.8m. This is observational cohort evidence, not proof that automation itself creates edge.
- 44.1% of traders were classified as topic specialists (>60% of categorized markets in one topic, at least five categorized markets).
- Specialization is heterogeneous: specialists overall were slightly less often profitable than generalists (28.1% vs 30.4%), driven by sports/politics/culture; finance specialists were 36.8% profitable and tech/science specialists 41.2% in the reported sample.
- Profitable accounts had larger median positions ($13.96 vs $10), but activity/confounding prevents interpreting sizing as a causal edge.
- Holding period did not show a clean robust relationship with profitability after activity stratification.
- Important provenance limits: international Polymarket only; full-history data spans different fee regimes; wallets are not unique persons; automated-account classification is heuristic.
