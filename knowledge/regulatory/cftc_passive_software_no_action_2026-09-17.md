# CFTC passive-software no-action position — 2026-09-17

Status: WATCH / infrastructure-access regime evidence, not an economic edge.

## Observation
On 2026-09-17 the CFTC Market Participants Division issued a broadly available no-action position for providers of passive software. Subject to specified conditions, staff will not recommend enforcement for failure to register as an introducing broker or associated person solely for providing/marketing software that facilitates users trading with registered FCMs, IBs and DCMs. The CFTC states this is similar to Staff Letter 26-09 (Phantom) but broadly available.

Primary sources:
- https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9300-26
- CFTC Staff Letter 26-25, issuance date 2026-09-17.

## Research interpretation
This can lower a regulatory/distribution barrier for third-party software front ends connecting users to registered derivatives/prediction-market infrastructure. It is therefore a venue-access/ecosystem WATCH trigger. It does not establish a pricing anomaly, signal advantage, queue advantage, lower realized fees, or positive expected value.

## Failure memory / guardrail
Do not infer `more software distribution -> more liquidity -> exploitable edge`. Direction and magnitude of any liquidity/spread/competition effect require point-in-time executable evidence. It may just as plausibly increase competition and compress existing opportunities.

Signal edge: not established.
Market edge: not established.
Execution edge: not established.
Economic conclusion: NO_PROVEN_EDGE.
