# CFTC mention-market advisory — 2026-09-23

Status: `WATCH / NO_PROVEN_EDGE`

## Observation
On 2026-09-22 the CFTC Division of Market Oversight issued Staff Advisory 26-27 covering event contracts whose settlement depends on whether a person says or mentions words, attends/appears at an event, or otherwise interacts with another person. The CFTC states that these contracts present heightened manipulation risk because settlement can turn on discrete conduct that may be neither independently generated nor externally verifiable. The advisory describes limited circumstances in which such contracts may be listed consistently with the CEA and Commission regulations and reminds DCMs of Core Principle 3 and Part 40 obligations.

Primary provenance:
- CFTC press release 9302-26, 2026-09-22: https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9302-26
- CFTC Staff Advisory 26-27 landing page: https://www.cftc.gov/node/260241

## Inference
This is a material rules/listing-regime WATCH trigger for mention/appearance/interaction contracts. It may affect which products are listed, how narrowly they are specified, what settlement/source controls are required, and how venues monitor or constrain manipulation susceptibility.

It is not evidence of a signal, market, or execution edge. A venue/product change must be observed point-in-time before any economics are reconsidered.

## Red-team guardrail
Do not convert `heightened manipulation risk` into an operational trading thesis based on influencing the underlying event or exploiting non-public/control information. Such conduct is outside project scope and may violate exchange rules or law. Only passive, public, lawful observation of resulting rule/product/liquidity changes is research-eligible.
