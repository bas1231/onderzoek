## Observation

On 2026-09-22 the CFTC Division of Market Oversight issued an advisory specifically covering event contracts whose settlement depends on whether an individual says or mentions words, attends/appears at an event, or otherwise interacts with another person. CFTC Release 9302-26 states that these contracts present a heightened manipulation risk because settlement can turn on discrete conduct of a person that may be neither independently generated nor externally verifiable.

The CFTC says only limited circumstances may permit such contracts to be listed consistently with the Commodity Exchange Act and Commission regulations. It reminds DCMs that Core Principle 3 requires contracts not to be readily susceptible to manipulation and calls for complete contract-specific Part 40 analysis.

Primary source: CFTC Release 9302-26, 2026-09-22, https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9302-26

A same-day secondary report adds that DCMs are expected to consider whether the person controlling the settlement-relevant conduct is constrained by independent legal/professional/contractual/fiduciary/confidentiality/organizational obligations and to use proactive trading rules and controls. This detail should be treated as secondary until the underlying advisory text is archived directly.

Secondary source: The Block, 2026-09-22, `CFTC warns 'mention market' prediction contracts carry heightened manipulation risk`.
