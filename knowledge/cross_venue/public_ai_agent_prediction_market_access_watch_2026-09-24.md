## Observation
Public launched Prediction Markets with AI Agents on 2026-09-24. Public's own product page says agents can monitor event-contract implied probabilities, automatically buy/sell event contracts under user-defined conditions, and use event-contract probabilities to trigger actions elsewhere in a portfolio. Public's launch announcement says the initial focus includes crypto, commodities, climate, economics, corporate events, markets/indices, tech/science, and politics/elections. Reporting describes the launch as tied to Kalshi.

## Inference
This is a material participant-access/automation change, not evidence of an edge. It may alter participant mix and reaction dynamics in financially linked event markets by making threshold-based monitoring/execution easier for retail brokerage users and by coupling prediction-market probabilities to other portfolio actions.

This is a WATCH trigger for informed-flow, behavioral, and microstructure research only.

## Red-team quick kill
Blocked inference: `AI agents available -> exploitable predictable flow`.
Reasons: users choose heterogeneous conditions; routing/aggregation may hide origin; other participants can adapt; headline automation does not establish volume share, latency, adverse selection, or persistence. Public's own product description is capability evidence, not execution-edge evidence.

## Recheck trigger / decisive test
Recheck only when at least one of the following becomes public and reproducible:
- route/venue-specific volume or participant-mix evidence after launch;
- synchronized L2/trade data around common probability thresholds with a preregistered pre/post design;
- Public/Kalshi primary documentation materially changing order types, fees, routing, access, or API behavior;

A valid test must separate signal edge, market edge and execution edge, and include executable bid/ask, depth, fees, fills/markouts and opportunity lifetime. No live trading is required.
