# Recon WATCH specialist triage V12

A WATCH finding is routed to the specialists named by its existing `falsification.specialist_route` as a triage-only research item.

WATCH does not enter the killer/proof chain, does not gain promotion authority, and keeps `NO_PROVEN_EDGE`. DISCOVER remains audit-only. HUNT continues to use the existing HUNT route and prebuild-killer gate.

The triage asks specialists to establish the mechanism, public trigger, decisive falsification test, point-in-time validity, and net executable economics before any later HUNT promotion can be justified.

All live trading, paid actions, and wallet actions remain disabled.
